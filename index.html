Strategy

Approach

Every position follows a systematic, rules-based process: technical structure sets the entry and exit, and position size is set before the trade, not after. Risk on any single trade is capped at a fixed dollar limit or 2% of account value, whichever is lower.

All capital is corporate and proprietary — no outside investors, no managed accounts, no leverage beyond what the broker provides on the instrument itself.

Principles

  • Defined entry and exit rules, set before the trade
  • Fixed dollar cap or 2% of account value per trade, whichever is lower
  • Liquid, exchange-traded futures only
  • Position size follows risk, not conviction
  • Monthly reporting, continuously verified
  • Strategy reviewed against its own trading record on an ongoing basis

About

KingdomOak Capital exists to test a simple claim: that systematic, disciplined trading holds up when it's backed by real risk management and shown in public, month after month.

I run it as a federally incorporated business, trading exclusively with corporate capital, and I specialize in exchange-traded futures — index, metals, energy, currency, and crypto contracts that make precise risk control possible at any account size.

I also document the process on a YouTube channel, Trading in Progress — the methodology, the market reads, and the discipline that a public track record actually requires.

Electrical engineer by profession, trader by specialization.
